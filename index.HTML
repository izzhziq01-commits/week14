The procrastination process has been trained into student life for years, so I'm sure you're familiar with the story.

An assignment comes in.

You tell yourself,
"I still have time"

You doomscrolling and
call it research.

You enter the 9PM sprint
and panic.

At 11:59 PM, you submit and
call it a miracle.

Why procrastination
doesn't work?

  • It turns learning into survival You are no longer learning calmly.
    You are simply trying to make it out alive before 11:59 PM.
  • It steals your recovery time Even when the task is submitted, the cost remains.
    Less sleep, more stress, and barely any energy left for what comes next
  • It makes panic feel productive The pressure feels powerful, but that doesn't mean it works.
    Panic creates urgency, not quality.
